How the lab works & data limitations

This is a local paper-trading sandbox using the repository’s historical Nasdaq CSV exports, ending in December 2024. Prices are source values, may be unadjusted for splits and dividends, and are rounded to cents for fills. Results exclude fees, slippage, taxes, dividends, and corporate actions; discontinuities can distort comparisons. These are toy strategies, not forecasts.

You trade at the currently revealed close. Trend follower buys when the 5 day average exceeds the 20 day average and exits when it falls below. Dip buyer buys 3% below the 20 day average and exits at that average or after a 10% decline from its entry. Both use the previous day’s signal and the next day’s opening price. Buy & hold buys at the first close. The final day remains available for manual trades.

Cash, positions, replay day, and bot progress are restored per market. Playback always resumes paused. Reset clears only the selected market. Arcade credits are separate and reset when the page reloads.
