## Explicit model limits

Historical source data ends in December 2024. It may be unadjusted for splits/dividends; discontinuities can distort results. No corporate-action adjustment, dividends, transaction fees, slippage, taxes, live quotes, or real-money transactions are modeled. The app exposes these limits. This release does not claim to be an investment backtester.

Manual orders fill at the revealed close. Bots see the previous close and trade at the next open; gaps can carry fills beyond their signal thresholds. Buy & hold buys at the first close. Portfolio value includes remaining cash and marked-to-market holdings; there is no forced end liquidation. The last date remains available for manual trades.

The journal stores up to 10,000 decisions per market and shows the latest 100. Browser storage is local convenience, not a secure account or multi-device service. Arcade credits and rounds reset on reload; they never affect market balances.
